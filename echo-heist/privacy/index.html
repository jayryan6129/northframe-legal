Echo Heist
Privacy Policy.

How we handle local saves, age-range choices, advertising information and privacy requests.

Effective October 22, 2026Last updated October 22, 2026Northframe Technologies

This Privacy Policy explains the information handled by Echo Heist, the advertising services included in the Android app and your choices. Core gameplay and saved progress work locally. Advertising services and support communications involve the third parties described below.

01

Information You Provide

Echo Heist is an Android memory and strategy game developed by Northframe Technologies (Android package com.echoheist.game). This policy also describes the local browser preview and this public policy page.

The game does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, payment-card information or precise GPS location to play. It has no player chat or public profile. The current release has no real-money purchases.

Android releases from version 0.7.1 ask for a broad age range to apply the advertising controls described below. We do not ask for your full birth date or identity documents. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

02

Local Game Data

Echo Heist saves the following information in app storage on your device, or in your browser's site storage when using the preview:

  • Earned credits, unlocked cosmetic outfits and your equipped outfit
  • Tutorial completion, completed-contract counts used for ad pacing, and game records such as scores, rooms, moves, mistakes and accuracy
  • Sound, graphics, reduced-motion and haptic preferences
  • On Android, your broad age-range choice and advertising-consent preferences managed by Google's consent tools

We use this information to restore your game, retain preferences and apply privacy and advertising choices. Northframe Technologies does not operate an Echo Heist account server, cloud-save service or remote score database in this release. Local gameplay saves and the broad age-range choice are not uploaded to a Northframe server. Google may process advertising-related information separately as described in this policy.

Android app backup is disabled. Echo Heist does not intentionally back up this local gameplay state through Android Auto Backup.

03

Advertising

The Android app includes Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. From version 0.7.1, the app starts its consent and ad-request flow only after the player selects 18 or older. Selecting Under 18, Prefer not to say, or leaving the range unspecified keeps ad requests off. An adult age selection is not consent to personalized advertising; Google's applicable privacy choices remain separate.

  • Banners may appear in headquarters, the collection and results. Their separate native surface collapses during gameplay.
  • Rewarded videos are optional on a cleared-vault report. A verified completion doubles that room's original cosmetic credits once. The optional cipher bonus stays separate. Normal play does not require watching a video.
  • Interstitials may appear when leaving a later, successfully completed three-room campaign or daily contract report. The first completed contract skips the intermission. Requests are limited by a five-minute interval after startup and fullscreen ads.

Credits unlock cosmetic outfits and do not improve shields, abilities or scores. The game does not use app-open ads. Ad availability and the type of ads served depend on consent status, region, device settings, connection, inventory and Google's policies.

Development builds use Google's sample ads and generate no advertising revenue. Test mode is not a promise that the advertising SDK processes no device information. The browser preview does not request ads or collect an age-range choice.

04

Data Processed by Google

Google's Mobile Ads disclosure describes processing that can include:

  • IP address and general location: the IP address can be used to infer an approximate area
  • Interactions: launches, taps, video views and engagement with ads
  • Diagnostics: technical information about app and SDK operation and performance
  • Identifiers: advertising ID, App Set ID and other applicable device or account identifiers, subject to configuration and user controls

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and protection against fraud and abuse. Google states that data sent by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Northframe Technologies can receive AdMob reports about requests, impressions, estimated revenue and related advertising performance.

Echo Heist does not add a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service in this release. This does not mean that Google's advertising SDK performs no measurement or diagnostics.

See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

05

Age Range, Consent and Privacy Choices

Your age-range selection is stored on the device and can be changed in the game's settings. No full birth date is requested. This is a self-declared privacy setting, not identity or age verification. Unspecified age does not qualify for ad requests.

For adult players, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent status and present applicable consent or opt-out messages when required and available. Ad requests are gated by UMP's canRequestAds() result. Depending on the region, the provider's message can offer consent, refusal, management of purposes and partners, or applicable US state opt-out choices.

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Ad privacy options is available in settings so adult players can revisit their choices. A permanent Privacy policy link is also available. These controls are separate from your age-range selection.

If a previously adult selection is changed to Under 18 or Prefer not to say, the app stops further ad requests and removes its ad inventory. Information already processed by Google remains subject to Google's policies and controls. Changing a setting does not retroactively erase information already sent.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and related privacy options through Android and Google settings. Depending on applicable law and service configuration, declining personalization may permit limited or contextual advertising; it does not necessarily prevent every advertising-related request for an adult player.

06

Sharing, Hosting and Support

Northframe Technologies does not sell your locally saved Echo Heist progress or operate a server that receives those saves. Advertising information may be sent to Google through the SDK and used as described above. Applicable privacy messages and provider controls explain relevant advertising choices.

If you contact us, we use the information in your message to respond and handle your request. Email is processed by our email provider. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents to game support.

This privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser/request metadata, to the hosting provider. This page has no advertising, tracking scripts, external fonts or submission forms. GitHub's own processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Opening links to other services is subject to those services' policies.

07

Retention, Deletion and Your Rights

Local game progress, preferences and the Android age-range choice remain until overwritten, cleared or removed with the app. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Echo Heist → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by device), or uninstall the game. This removes local progress, outfits, records and preferences. Browser-preview users can clear site data for the preview's origin. The game has no account-deletion process because it has no player accounts.

Google retains and deletes information it processes according to its own policies, legal duties, product configuration and user settings. Clearing Echo Heist's local storage does not necessarily delete information already held by Google. Refer to Google's privacy controls for that information.

We retain support correspondence as needed to respond to and resolve the request, and for applicable legal obligations. Depending on where you live and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent. Contact us using the address below to make a request about information we handle. We may need limited information to verify and respond to the request. Requests concerning Google's separate processing may need to be directed to Google.

08

Security and International Processing

Game saves are kept in the app's local storage or the preview browser's site storage. We restrict the Android game's embedded browser to bundled game assets; the public privacy policy opens separately. Advertising networking uses the SDK's transport protections, including Google's documented TLS encryption. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google, GitHub and email providers may process information on systems outside your country under their applicable policies and safeguards. Northframe Technologies does not promise that these providers store information in a particular country.

09

Intended Audience and Younger Players

Echo Heist is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13. This intended-audience statement is separate from any content rating assigned by a store or regional rating authority.

From Android version 0.7.1, players selecting Under 18, Prefer not to say, or no age range can play without ad requests. Adult players still receive the separate applicable advertising-consent flow. These controls do not verify a person's age or replace applicable platform and local privacy requirements.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about a child has been provided to us, please contact us. Information received through support can be reviewed for appropriate removal; information handled separately by service providers is subject to their controls and legal obligations.

10

We may update this policy when Echo Heist, its services, data practices or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy at this URL and update the revision date. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate notices where required. The age-range and adult-only ad-request controls described here apply from Android version 0.7.1; earlier development builds may not include that choice.
