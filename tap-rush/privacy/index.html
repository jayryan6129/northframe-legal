Privacy Policy for Tap Rush

Developed by Northframe Technologies

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Northframe Technologies respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Tap Rush handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Tap Rush does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share personal information.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the game.

2. Local Game Data

Tap Rush may store limited game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • High score
  • Game progress
  • Best level reached
  • Sound preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Northframe Technologies or any external server.

3. Advertising

Tap Rush does not currently contain advertisements or advertising software development kits (SDKs).

4. Analytics and Tracking

Tap Rush does not currently use analytics services, behavioral tracking technologies, or third-party tracking SDKs.

5. Internet Access

The current version of Tap Rush does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay functionality.

6. Sharing of Information

Because Tap Rush does not collect personal information, Northframe Technologies does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Tap Rush does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Data Deletion

Game information stored locally by Tap Rush can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application from the device.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Northframe Technologies may update this Privacy Policy when the application changes or when required by applicable policies or regulations.

Any updates will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
