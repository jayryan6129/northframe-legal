Tap Rush · Privacy

Privacy built into the Tap Rush experience.

This policy explains how Tap Rush handles local game data, advertising, consent choices, and third-party services used to support the game.

Effective August 12, 2026 Last updated August 12, 2026 Developer: Northframe Technologies

Northframe Technologies respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Tap Rush handles information, how advertising services are used, and the privacy choices available to users.

01

Information You Provide

Tap Rush does not require a user account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, payment-card information, or precise location in order to play the game.

02

Local Game Data

Tap Rush stores limited game-related information locally on the user's device. This may include:

  • Best score and highest level reached
  • Gameplay statistics and local progression information
  • Music, sound-effect, and haptic preferences
  • Tutorial-completion status
  • Local counters and timestamps used to keep interstitial ads infrequent

This game data is used to provide Tap Rush functionality and remains locally on the device. Northframe Technologies does not operate a Tap Rush user-account, cloud-save, or remote score database for this release.

03

Advertising

Tap Rush uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display a limited number of advertisements.

  • Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only when a user chooses to watch an ad in exchange for a clearly described gameplay benefit, such as an additional life or a revive.
  • Interstitial ads may appear at natural Game Over transitions. Tap Rush intentionally limits their frequency and does not display interstitial ads during active gameplay.
  • Tap Rush does not use banner ads, app-open ads, or forced ads between normal gameplay levels in this release.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on user choices, device settings, region, applicable laws, and Google's advertising policies.

04

Data Processed by Google Mobile Ads

Google states that the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on device settings and availability, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • User product interactions, including app and advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available and permitted

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Northframe Technologies may receive aggregated advertising and performance reports through Google AdMob, such as ad requests, impressions, interactions, estimated earnings, and related performance metrics.

For additional information about Google's data practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

05

Consent and Privacy Choices

Tap Rush uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising-related privacy choices where required. When a privacy options entry point is required, Tap Rush provides a Privacy Choices option in the app so users can review or change applicable choices.

Users may also manage or reset advertising identifiers and related privacy controls through their Android device and Google settings where available.

06

Data Sharing

Northframe Technologies does not sell users' locally stored Tap Rush game data. Advertising-related information may be processed or shared with Google as described above in order to provide advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention functionality.

07

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored Tap Rush game data remains on the device until it is overwritten by normal game use, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Northframe Technologies does not maintain a Tap Rush account database containing this local gameplay information. Data processed by Google through Google Mobile Ads is retained and handled according to Google's applicable policies and user settings.

08

Security

Tap Rush does not operate a remote game-account or cloud-score database in this release. Advertising-related network requests rely on the security mechanisms provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, including encrypted transport as documented by Google.

09

Children's Privacy

Tap Rush does not require users to provide personal information and does not provide a user-account system. The intended Google Play audience for this release begins at age 13. Advertising and privacy functionality will be configured in accordance with the audience selected in Google Play Console and applicable Google policies.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Northframe Technologies may update this Privacy Policy when Tap Rush, its advertising functionality, privacy features, applicable laws, or platform requirements change. Any revision will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
